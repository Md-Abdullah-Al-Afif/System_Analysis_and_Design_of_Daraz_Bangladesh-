## Project Overview

This project investigates Daraz Bangladesh, the country's largest online e-commerce marketplace (part of the Alibaba-owned Daraz Group), to identify systemic challenges and engineer a robust, modernized system. 

### Key Problems Identified
1. **Product Authenticity & Quality Control:** Inconsistent verification of third-party marketplace sellers leading to counterfeit or damaged goods.
2. **Refund & Return Process:** Opaque, prolonged refund timelines and lack of immediate return confirmation for customers (especially prevalent in Cash on Delivery transactions).
3. **Account Security & Fraud:** Vulnerabilities regarding unauthorized account access (e.g., gift card skimming) and external brand impersonation/fake websites.
4. **Delivery Service:** Last-mile delivery delays and mislabeled "failed delivery attempts" without verifiable proof.
5. **Customer Support & Seller Communication:** Heavy reliance on unyielding automated chatbots and slow, inconsistent seller response times.

### Proposed Redesigned System (Candidate System)
To resolve these core challenges, the project proposes a comprehensive system upgrade featuring:
* **Seller Verification & Trust Scoring:** Mandatory document uploads (NID/trade licenses) and a visible Trust Score for every merchant prior to purchase.
* **Fraud Detection & OTP Verification:** Risk-scoring engines that trigger multi-factor SMS OTP confirmation during suspicious logins or high-value checkouts.
* **Digital Return Receipts & Tracking:** Instant generation of trackable digital receipts upon initiating a product return.
* **Delivery Proof Code & Rider Logging:** Mandatory delivery OTP codes and timestamped call/photo logs to eliminate false "failed delivery" disputes.
* **Support Ticket SLAs & Escalation:** Automatic routing of unresolved chatbot queries to human support agents backed by strict Service-Level Agreement (SLA) deadlines.
